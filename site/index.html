Questions

Good to know
Is the cash included in the price?

Yes. Money bouquet prices are the total: the $1 bills, roses, wrapping, butterflies and your custom topper are all included. No hidden extras.

How far in advance should I order?

Please order at least 3–5 days ahead. Need it sooner? Rush orders are available for an extra $35 when the schedule allows.

How do I secure my order?

A 50% non-refundable deposit locks in your date. The balance is due before pickup or delivery. We accept Apple Pay and Zelle.

Do you deliver?

Yes! Both pickup and delivery are available. Choose your option on the order form. For privacy, the pickup address is shared once your order is confirmed.

Can I customize colors and the topper?

Absolutely. Pick your colors, ribbon or topper text, and add touches like butterflies, chocolates or LED lights. Just note it in your order.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

All sales are final and deposits are non-refundable, since every bouquet is handmade just for you.
